Putting it together – simple “normal vs abnormal echo” logic

If you were designing a rule-based classifier from a report like this, you might:

Label as “Normal heart/echo” if:
	1.	LV size & systolic function normal (EF in normal range, no RWMA)
	2.	Diastolic function normal
	3.	RV size & function normal
	4.	LA & RA normal
	5.	No more than trivial valvular regurg, no stenosis
	6.	PASP within normal range
	7.	No septal defects, masses, or pericardial effusion

Label as “Abnormal heart/echo” if any of the following present:
	•	LV systolic dysfunction, LVH or dilatation, RWMA
	•	Any grade of diastolic dysfunction
	•	Chamber enlargement (LA, RA, LV, RV)
	•	More than trivial valvular regurgitation or any stenosis
	•	Elevated PASP
	•	Pericardial effusion, mass, thrombus, or shunt
